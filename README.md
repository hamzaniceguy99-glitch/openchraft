## Business description (Stripe, directories…)

```
Coaching in leatherworking, delivered online: one-on-one video sessions and small group sessions covering tools and leather selection, cutting and saddle stitching, edge finishing and dyeing, and project work from small goods to bags. Makers follow an 8 to 12 week program with photo review of the pieces they complete. We sell no leather, tools or kits and take no supplier commission. Services are sold as month-to-month subscriptions from $25 to $139 per month, cancellable at any time. No physical products are sold or shipped. Site: openchraft.shop
```
